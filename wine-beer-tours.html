Wine & Beer Tours

Virginia wine country,
nobody drives.

Private winery and brewery tours through Charlottesville and the Blue Ridge region.

The Charlottesville area sits at the center of one of Virginia's most celebrated wine and craft beer corridors. Golden Limousine builds fully private, custom-routed tours so your group experiences every stop without anyone giving up the day to drive.

We work with you in advance to plan a route around your preferences — whether that means a focused afternoon at two or three standout wineries, or a full-day sweep of the Blue Ridge trail. Bachelorette parties, birthday groups, corporate hospitality outings, and private couples excursions all travel with the same level of care and privacy.

No shared vehicles. No strangers. No preset departure times. Your chauffeur adapts to the pace of your group throughout the day, and returns everyone home safely at the end of it.

  • Fully private custom itineraries — no group tours, no shared vehicles
  • Charlottesville-area wineries and Blue Ridge wine trail routes
  • Craft brewery and taproom circuits throughout Central Virginia
  • Bachelorette, birthday, and private celebration packages
  • Corporate and client hospitality outings
  • Half-day (4 hrs) and full-day (8 hrs) options available
  • Door-to-door from your hotel, rental, or home
