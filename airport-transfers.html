Airport & Cruise Terminal Transfers

Every flight, met
on time and ready.

Flight-tracked chauffeur service from Charlottesville to every major airport in the region.

Golden Limousine provides premium airport transfer service for travelers departing from or arriving into the Charlottesville, Harrisonburg, and greater Central Virginia area. Whether you are headed to Dulles for an international departure or catching a regional flight out of CHO, we handle the logistics so you can focus on travel.

Your chauffeur monitors your flight from departure to touchdown. Early landings, delays, gate changes — we adjust in real time without a call from you. You clear baggage claim and your driver is waiting, name sign in hand.

We also serve cruise terminal transfers to Baltimore and the greater Mid-Atlantic region. Flat-rate pricing on every run, confirmed in advance, with no surge charges or surprise fees.
