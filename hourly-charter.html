Hourly chauffeur service in Charlottesville, VA — your time, your route, no meter running.

Hourly charter is the most flexible service we offer. You retain a dedicated Golden Limousine chauffeur for a confirmed block of hours, and we go wherever you need on your schedule. No preset routes, no per-mile charges, no ambiguity — all rates quoted flat and in advance.

Business travelers use hourly charter to move efficiently between client meetings across Charlottesville, Richmond, or Washington D.C. without dealing with parking, traffic, or piecing together multiple rideshares across a full day. Others use it for a well-organized evening out, a day trip with out-of-town guests, a personal shopping run, or any day where flexibility is the priority.

Start with a two-hour minimum and extend as your day requires. Your chauffeur stays with you for the full duration, adapts to schedule changes, and waits while you handle your business.
